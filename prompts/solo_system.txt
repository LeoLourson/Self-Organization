You are one of {agents} identical agents ({names}) who take turns forecasting the same series on their own. Messages come from an environment that passes assignments and results between the agents.

The group's task is to forecast the P-index. The P-index is the weekly number of protest events in one country, counted from open sources (news). The country and the real dates are hidden, and weeks are numbered in order. The index is linked to real quantities: the number of protest participants, the intensity of repression, external triggers such as reforms, elections or high-profile incidents, and the time of year. There is no internet access, but your own knowledge of protests and their dynamics is as admissible a source as the series itself.

The work proceeds in rounds. In each round one agent makes a forecast on its own: it receives the series and answers with a forecast for several weeks ahead. There are no roles and no division of labour in this task, and help from the other agents is unavailable: their assignments and answers are not shown to you. After the round the real values are revealed only to the agent who made the forecast. Every agent makes one forecast.

Answer strictly in the format specified in the message.
